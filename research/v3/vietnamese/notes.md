# Vietnamese shortlist (v3): notes, 15/10/2026

The 14-venue v2 list was cut to 4. Ranked best-first:

1. **Bánh Tráng Trường Hoàng**: northern-style cuốn thịt heo (pork rice-paper rolls)
2. **Bếp Tự Tâm**: home-style cơm nhà with a nostalgic interior
3. **Nét Cuốn**: bánh xèo, bánh khọt and central/southern cuốn
4. **Vua Chả Cá AEON**: chả cá, the only in-AEON pick

## Hard filters applied
- **No hotpot or grill.** This removed:
  - Bò An Khang (hotpot)
  - Sành Lẩu
  - A Choén (charcoal-grilled beef)
  - Phương Nam AEON (hotpot-focused, 4.0★)
  - Kim Kê Quán
  - Lẩu Nấm Gia Khánh
  - Sườn Mười / Sườn Kingdom (BBQ ribs)
- **Price roughly 150–400k per person.** Cheap breakfast-style shops were excluded: Ngự Uyển bún bò Huế (~50–100k), Bún chả 27 (<100k, closes 19:30), and Bún Riêu Mai Cua.
- **Chả cá is borderline.** It is included only as the AEON pick, because it is clearly the best Vietnamese option inside the mall. The alternatives there are Hàng Cuốn 4.0★, Phương Nam 4.0★ and Nét Huế 2.7★. Bò Tơ Quán Mộc AEON (4.8★) was weighed against it, but recent reviews describe a 1-hour wait and dry steamed beef, and its menu leans toward hotpot and combos.

## Why these four
- **Trường Hoàng (cuốn).** It has 4.9★ from 1,377 reviews, a new, clean and quiet space, and costs 200–300k.
  - Chosen over **Phú Cường**: 4.8★, but 300–500k, with a recent review saying "thỉnh thoảng thịt hôi" (the meat sometimes smells off) and a kids' play area.
  - Caveat: one 07/2026 review says portions are small for the price and the dipping sauce is sweet.
- **Bếp Tự Tâm (cơm nhà).** It has the most photogenic "Vietnamese home meal" setting and costs 200–300k.
  - It **closes at 21:00**, so it only works for dinner before the film.
  - One review says it is cramped and the kho quẹt (dipping sauce) is salty.
- **Nét Cuốn (bánh xèo / central cuốn).** It covers bánh xèo and is different in style from Trường Hoàng. The shop is cosy for two, has an active Facebook page, and has a review photo dated 14/10/2026.
  - Chosen over **Bếp Xì Khói** (bánh xèo tôm nhảy): a ~04/2026 review reports dirty rice-paper dipping water reused between customers. That is a hygiene red flag.
- **Vua Chả Cá AEON (chả cá).** It means zero travel and is very active: the owner posted 3 days ago and the newest review photo is from 14/10/2026.
  - The 4.9★ from 4.7k reviews is probably boosted by a promotional chain.
  - Some reviews complain about service, and clothes may pick up some cooking smell.
- **Not found:** no upscale bún/phở venue in the area met the bar. Phở Thìn, Lý Quốc Sư and Bát Đá are all 3.9–4.2★ casual shops.
