# Western: v3 shortlist (curated 15/10/2026, for Sat 17/10/2026 evening)

Rules applied: pizza, pasta, steak or bistro. Steak must be plated in the kitchen; no tabletop grills. Date-appropriate space, about 150–400k per person, with steak allowed a little higher.

## Picks (best first)
1. **Pizza 4P's, AEON Mall Hà Đông, floor 1.**
   - Google 4.9 from 14,291 reviews. Cave-themed room with a wood-fired oven, in the same building as CGV.
   - Saturday evenings are very busy. Reviews mention waiting more than an hour for a table without a booking.
   - **Book ahead:** booking.pizza4ps.com or 1900 6043.
2. **Le Monde Steak, 161 Nguyễn Văn Lộc.**
   - Google 4.6 from 539 reviews. French-bistro steak sets; the chain refreshed its brand and interiors in 2025.
   - Saturday hours: 10:30–14:30 and 17:00–22:00.
   - **Caveat:** lemondesteak.vn still shows "Account Suspended" when checked on 15/10/2026.
   - Evidence that it is open:
     - the chain fanpage is live (95k followers, 336 people talking about it);
     - Lemon8 posts from 8/2025 and 11/2025 cover the rebrand;
     - Google reviews are 2–4 months old;
     - the Google listing has photos of the new storefront and interior.
   - Call 0886 083 663 to confirm.
3. **L'amour Steak, New Skyline, Văn Quán.**
   - Google 4.5 from 352 reviews. About 15 minutes away.
   - The most romantic room of the three: wine-rack walls, roses and warm light. Offers anniversary table setups.
   - **Caveat:** food reviews are mixed. One from 8/2026 found the bread hard and a bill of more than 800k poor value. Others praise the T-bone.
