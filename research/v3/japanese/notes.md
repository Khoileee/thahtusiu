# Japanese shortlist (v3): notes, 15/10/2026

The 14-venue v2 list was cut to 4. Ranked best-first:

1. **Nama Sushi Nguyễn Văn Lộc**: sushi
2. **IPPUDO AEON**: ramen, the only in-AEON pick
3. **Koichan (Văn Phú)**: a general Japanese restaurant with hot dishes
4. **Sushibar Hà Đông**: sushi/sashimi with fancier presentation

## Hard filters applied
- **No grill/BBQ and no hotpot.** This removed Shogun (charcoal BBQ), both Yakimono branches, Yakitori 18, and iSushi (a buffet that includes grill and hotpot). Chibo (teppan okonomiyaki) was also dropped, because the AEON slot went to Ippudo.
- **Price roughly 150–400k per person.**
  - Hadu Sushi is excluded: Google now shows 500–600k, with some answers at 900k–1tr.
  - SAKU is excluded: under 100k, cramped, and a recent review complains that quality has dropped.
  - Darumen and Kyoto Sushi are excluded as small and cheap.
- **At most 1 in-AEON venue.** That slot went to Ippudo (4.9★, opened 08/2026). Tokyo Tantan is a food-court counter. Chibo would be the second AEON pick.

## Why these four
- **Nama** has the best balance: 300–400k, a big, clean, warm-lit space, and a review that literally says "rất hợp ăn tối hẹn hò" (very suitable for a date dinner). Its newest review with photos is dated 13/10/2026.
- **Ippudo** needs zero travel and is a proper sit-down restaurant. Its reviews from the last 1–2 months are strong, and one reviewer mentions eating there right after a movie. **Hours caveat:** Google shows 10:00–21:00 for Saturday 17/10, while AEON says 09:00–22:00. Eat before the film.
- **Koichan** covers the "nice Japanese restaurant, not grill" slot. It has donburi, okonomiyaki and sashimi, 4.9★ from 496 reviews, a review photo dated 10/10/2026, and an active Facebook page. Confidence is medium because few sources cover it.
  - **Distance correction:** v2 said ~2.5 km straight-line. Recomputed from Google coordinates it is ~3.9 km straight-line, or about 5 km and 12–15 minutes by road.
- **Sushibar** is the more romantic, photogenic sushi option. It ranks last because it is pricier (Google says 400–500k; one guest paid ~500k for 2) and its 5.0★ looks inflated: a review mentions getting a free item.

## Sources: what was reachable
- **Google Maps place data (live, 15/10/2026): reachable.** Used for ratings, hours for Saturday 17/10, price bands, review text with relative dates, dates of review photos, and photos.
  - The newest-first review RPC returned **403**, so review dates are approximate ("1 tháng trước" = 09/2026, i.e. "1 month ago").
- **Facebook: partially reachable.** Only public page metadata loaded: follower counts and "người đang nói về điều này" ("people talking about this", a recent-activity count). Posts and groups such as "Hà Đông ăn gì" need a login and could not be read.
- **TikTok: not reachable.** /discover pages returned a WAF stub or empty shell.
- **Lemon8: not reachable.** The search page is client-rendered with no data.
- **WebSearch (Foody, Wanderlog, news): very thin.** It found nothing branch-specific apart from Wanderlog's general Sushibar chain note.
- **AEON Mall Hà Đông directory:** confirms Ippudo at T155.
