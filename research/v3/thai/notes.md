# Thai: v3 shortlist (curated 15/10/2026, for Sat 17/10/2026 evening)

Rules applied:
- Thai à-la-carte only; places that mainly serve lẩu (hotpot) are excluded.
- Date-friendly space.
- Roughly 150–400k per person.

## Picks (best first)
1. **Thai Market, Vincom Mega Mall Smart City, floor 1.**
   - About 7–10 minutes from AEON. Google 4.8 from 407 reviews.
   - Recent reviews praise the cleanliness, the pad thái and the green curry.
   - Saturday hours are 10:30–21:30, so eat before the movie if the screening is late.
   - Cross-checked: thaimarket.com.vn lists this branch, and the chain fanpage is active.
2. **Khrua Baan Thai, inside AEON Mall Hà Đông.**
   - The only Thai pick inside AEON. Google 4.9 from 660 reviews; the score may be inflated by in-store prompts to leave a review.
   - One review from 9/2026 says it is weaker than the Liễu Giai branch.
   - Google lists no hours; assume mall hours, about 22:00.
   - Cross-checked: it has a page in the AEON store directory, and the chain website and fanpage are active.
3. **Saep Thai Cuisine, Geleximco Lê Trọng Tấn.**
   - A new Thai bistro, opened around mid-2026. White brick, a wine shelf and flowers on the tables make it the most date-like Thai space.
   - Google 4.7 from 83 reviews, all recent and positive ("rất chuẩn vị Thái").
   - **Caveat:** only Google Maps confirms it is open. No fanpage, Lemon8 post or press coverage was found, and Google lists no hours.
   - Call ahead. If there is no answer, swap in OMG Thai or Khrua Baan Thai 155 Nguyễn Văn Lộc.

## Excluded
- **Mainly lẩu (hotpot) or grill (user rule):** Thái La Thái La, Koh Yam Nguyễn Văn Lộc, Nymo, Thai Mor Fai, Lẩu HT, Đảo Bangkok.
- **ThaiExpress AEON:** Google 4.0 from 290. Recent reviews report 45-minute waits and forgotten drinks at 8pm.
- **OMG Thai, Nguyễn Văn Trỗi (backup):** Google 4.5 from 637. Casual street-food style, and one recent review calls it poor value.
- **Khrua Baan Thai 155 Nguyễn Văn Lộc (backup):** Google 4.6 from 1,194, but it repeats the chain already picked. Recent reviews are mixed: too salty, small portions.
- **Yatts, Nam Trung Yên:** more than 20 minutes away and pricey. Reviews mention a damp smell and an unexpected parking fee.
- **Others:** MADEE (newest reviews are 3 years old), KOPKHUN (3.4), Bụi Thái (3.7), Yum In Thai (probably closed).
