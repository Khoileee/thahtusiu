# Korean: v3 shortlist (curated 15/10/2026, for Sat 17/10/2026 evening)

Rules applied:
- No hotpot or lẩu, including tokbokki hotpot buffets.
- No grill or BBQ.
- Date-appropriate space.
- Roughly 150–400k per person.
- Open status checked in more than one source where possible.

## Picks (best first)
1. **Pogeun, Nguyễn Văn Lộc.** Home-style à-la-carte Korean with cute pink decor. Google 4.8 from 1,173 reviews. Saturday hours are split: 10:15–15:00 and 17:00–22:00. Plan dinner around the movie and book on 0868 219 226.
2. **bb.q Chicken Hà Đông, The K-Park, Văn Phú.** Korean chain fried chicken, opened around 11/2025. Google 4.8 from 214 reviews; the shop is clean and new. Seating is on the 2nd floor and there is no lift.
3. **Don Chicken, The Linc, Park City.** The closest pick to AEON, about 8–10 minutes. Google 4.8 from 837 reviews; the space is roomy and clean.
   - **Caveat:** recent food reviews are mixed. Some say the chicken was dry or over-fried.
   - One review from around 6/2026 says "gà hôi, thịt không tươi".
