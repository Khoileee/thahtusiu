# Western / European food near AEON Mall Hà Đông: research notes

Research date: 15/10/2026. Target date: Saturday 17/10/2026.

## Method
- Ratings, review counts, hours and open/closed status were read directly from Google Maps place data on 15/10/2026, using place-specific queries. No ratings are invented. Where a value could not be found, it is null or left empty.
- Distances are straight-line from AEON Mall Hà Đông (20.989, 105.7515). Road distance and motorbike time are estimates.
- Photos come from official sites (pizza4ps.com, lapizzasteak.com, vanhoanggroup.com, aeonmall-hadong.com.vn), architecture press (kienviet.net) and PasGo. PasGo images are only 750x450 and carry a small PasGo watermark. No larger versions were available for L'amour (2 of 4 photos), Le Monde or Pepperonis.

## Ranking (best first)
1. Pizza 4P's Aeon Mall Hà Đông (inside AEON): Google 4.9 from 14,291 reviews. "Cave" interior. Best overall pick, and it is in the same building as CGV. Book ahead for Saturday evening (1900 6043).
2. L'amour Steak, Văn Quán: Google 4.5 from 352 reviews. Wine-cellar interior lit by candles. The most romantic option outside the mall. Current promo includes a free anniversary/birthday table setup.
3. La Pizza & Steak, Geleximco Lê Trọng Tấn: Google 4.8 from 462 reviews. Closest outside option (~10–12 min). New chain with an active website and fanpage in 2026.
4. Le Monde Steak, Nguyễn Văn Lộc: Google 4.6 from 539 reviews. Budget French-style steak. The chain website is suspended, so call ahead to confirm.
5. Al Fresco's Aeon Mall Hà Đông (inside AEON): Google 4.6 from 309 reviews. Backup inside the mall, with a family-restaurant feel.
6. Pepperonis Trần Phú: Google 4.6 from 1,634 reviews. Budget pizza and pasta, casual and noisy. Not very date-like.

## Exclusions and why
- **Le Monde Steak – AEON Mall Hà Đông**: a PasGo page exists (tầng 2), but the shop is absent from the official AEON Hà Đông store directory (API search on aeonmall-hadong.com.vn) and returns no Google Maps place. Likely closed or moved. Excluded.
- **Scorpioni Pizza Coffee Văn Khê** (Lô 13 BT9 KĐT Văn Khê): Google 4.3 from only 19 reviews, no opening hours, unclaimed listing, Google posting restricted. Mostly delivery according to older blog posts. Open status could not be verified. Excluded.
- **Pepper Lunch (AEON Hà Đông)**: open, but it is a Japanese teppan steak chain rather than Western, and a fast-casual format. Excluded from this category.
- **Pizza Hut / The Pizza Company / Domino's (AEON, Văn Phú)**: open, but mass-market chains and not date-appropriate. Note: Google shows Pizza Hut AEON at 5.0 from 5,184 reviews, which looks review-incentivised.
- **Pizza Me Drink Me (Văn Phú / Văn Quán)**, **Pizza Chảo (Văn Quán)**, **Jan Pizza & Mỳ Ý (Bà Triệu)**, **Pizza Ngon Hà Đông (Dương Nội)**: only found in old Foody or blog lists, with no Google Maps match and no recent evidence. Budget or delivery format. Excluded.
- **Bầu's Pizza Thanh Hà**: Google 4.5 from 33 reviews, takeaway-focused, ~7 km away. Not a date venue. Excluded.
- **Vietnamese-style bít tết shops** (Bít Tết Chính Hạnh, Ngọc Hiếu, Steak Land, Vic Steak 3.9 from 66, Hùng Mạnh): Vietnamese pan-steak (bò né) or family style rather than Western dining. Excluded.
- **"Prime Cut" and "Napoli" at The Linc ParkCity**: named in one aggregator article only. No Google Maps match. Possibly inaccurate content. Excluded.
- **D'art Bistro**: Google resolves it to Vinhomes Skylake, Cầu Giấy (~4.6 km away, a café). The Smart City listing could not be verified. Excluded.

## Caveats
- The Western dining scene right around AEON Hà Đông (Dương Nội, La Khê, Văn Phú, Park City) is thin. There is no independent bistro or brunch spot with strong, verifiable recent evidence. Every non-mall pick is 10–15 min away by motorbike (Văn Quán, Mộ Lao, Geleximco).
- Recent branch-specific review quotes were hard to obtain because Google review text was not retrievable. Quotes are from Tripadvisor, Lemon8 and Foody, each dated. Some are old (Pepperonis 2020, L'amour 2019 and 2024). La Pizza & Steak has no quotable independent review, so its highlights array is empty.
- Hours differ between Google, PasGo and official sites. For a Saturday-evening plan, call ahead.
- Since 1/7/2025, the old Hà Đông district has been reorganised into new wards, so official addresses may show new ward names.
