# Cafe research notes (14/10/2026)

## Method
- Candidates came from Google Maps viewport searches around AEON Mall Hà Đông, Dương Nội, Park City/La Khê, Văn Phú/Kiến Hưng, Thanh Hà and hồ Văn Quán (fetched 14/10/2026). Toidicafe's Hà Đông listing (API), Tripi (6/2025) and Khongsolac (5/2024) lists were cross-checked.
- Ratings, review counts, hours and "X tháng trước" review ages all come from Google Maps place data fetched 14/10/2026. The hours returned were dated for the week of 13–18/10/2026, which includes Saturday 17/10.
- Distances are straight-line × 1.35 at about 22 km/h by motorbike. These are estimates, not routed ETAs.
- All photos are Google Maps user photos (lh3.googleusercontent.com, `=w1600`), 1080–1600 px wide. Each one was checked visually on a contact sheet before selection. Two menu photos are saved as `menu.jpg` (Nhà Gỗ, Mộc Lab).
- TikTok/Instagram presence could not be verified: web search returned no venue-specific TikTok results, and no browser was available. `tiktok`/`instagram` are null unless a link appeared in Maps data.

## Excluded and why
- **Laika Cafe Văn Quán** (58BT8, view hồ, 4 floors + rooftop): open (reviews 3 months ago), but rated 3.6★ (687). Recent reviews complain about 30-minute waits, overly sweet/icy drinks and crowded ground-floor seating. Risky for a first date.
- **Rosé Café & Pub – Vườn Nhật Smart City**: 3.8★, ~3 km north across Đại lộ Thăng Long. A review says entering the Japanese garden needs a resident card. Most reviews are 1–2 years old.
- **Soranchi coffee** (Bảo Sơn, Japanese style): 4.0★. Further north (Tây Mỗ), and most reviews are 1–2 years old.
- **E'mu Café** (Dương Nội): 4.1★ with large sidewalk/bougainvillea seating. Maps shows conflicting hour sets, it is often used for car-launch events, and it got a cleanliness complaint.
- **Eden garden cafe & bistro** (58BT8 Văn Quán): its Maps listing has no review data in the fetch. It appears to share or overlap the AN Bistro/Laika site, so it was not listed separately.
- **Tropical Forest x Fordeer** (Văn Quán): not found on Google Maps by name, so its status couldn't be verified.
- **Nghiền Cafe Greenhouse** (Lacasta): 4.2★, but recent reviews are few and include a staff-yelling complaint. It also has a kids' play area, so less date-friendly.
- **Sen Cafe Văn Phú**: 3.9★, fans only (no AC), and complaints about staff attitude.
- **Kontact Coffee, Highlands Dương Nội, Hygge, I'm Hanoi, Kaana, Cafe Trắng, 1990 Coffee, Paradise Cafe**: open, but they are workspace or chain style without a notable view, or have weak ratings (Paradise 3.0, 1990 3.6). Kaana had no review data in the fetch.
- **KATINAT Phùng Hưng**: 3.3★, chain, no view.
- **Phúc Long (NewSkyline Văn Quán)**: 4.5★ (1,271), but it is a chain store in a building, and recent reviews complain about staff attitude. No Phúc Long inside AEON Hà Đông surfaced on Maps.
- **Meicha – Matcha Hà Đông** (7 LK6A Mộ Lao): 4.9★ (95), but its Maps data had no review ages or photos in the fetch. Space evidence is thin; prior research covers it.
- **Cheka**: kept, but note that reviews say it is very crowded on weekends.
- **AN Bistro**: kept, but flagged as pricey (about 80k/drink).

## Caveats
- **Amóre**: hours were not present in the Maps data. Confirm by phone or fanpage.
- Price ranges mix sources: Toidicafe (2022), menu photos and review mentions. Each entry says which source it uses.
- Văn Quán lake venues are about 17–18 minutes away (about 5.5–6 km). That is at the edge of the 15-minute target, so heavy Saturday-evening traffic on Tố Hữu / Nguyễn Trãi could push it past 20 minutes. The closest strong options are Kén Coffee (about 0.4 km), Kaki (about 0.7 km), Ucity (about 1.2 km) and Cheka (about 2 km).
