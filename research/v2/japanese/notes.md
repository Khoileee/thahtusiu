# Japanese food near AEON Mall Hà Đông: research notes (15/10/2026)

## Method
- **Ratings, review counts, hours, price bands, recent reviews and photos** come from Google Maps place data, fetched live on 15/10/2026. Every rating in venues.json is Google Maps.
- **"Still open" check:** each venue must show normal opening hours with no "permanently closed" flag. It must also have Google reviews from the last 1–4 months.
- **In-AEON venues** were also checked against the official store directory at aeonmall-hadong.com.vn/danh-muc-cua-hang/, which is current: it lists CoCo Ichibanya as opening in 06/2026.
- **Photos** are Google Maps user and owner photos, downloaded at width 1600 (`=w1600`). I checked each one by eye on a contact sheet. Street-view shots, menus, blurry images and TikTok screenshots were rejected.
- **Distances** are straight-line from AEON (20.9896, 105.7513) multiplied by about 1.35 for the road distance. Time assumes motorbike speed. On Saturday evening, Tố Hữu / Lê Trọng Tấn / Nguyễn Văn Lộc can add 3–5 minutes.

## Geography caveat
The only strong Japanese venue in Dương Nội, Park City, La Khê, Văn Phú or Kiến Hưng is Koichan (Văn Phú, about 3.4 km by road). It is included with medium confidence because there is no press coverage, no price data, and its newest review is about 3 months old. The quality cluster outside the mall is Nguyễn Văn Lộc / TSQ / Ao Sen in Mộ Lao, about 4–5.5 km from AEON (roughly 10–18 minutes by motorbike). The in-mall options are the only ones under 5 minutes away.

## Data caveats
- **Sushibar Hà Đông** shows 5.0★ on about 1.6k reviews. This is unusually high and may be inflated by incentivised reviews.
- **IPPUDO AEON:** the Google pin is at the wrong coordinates, but the address and the AEON directory confirm lot T155, floor 1. It opened around mid-2026.
- **Darumen:** Google Maps and Foody give no. 56, while the storefront photo and an older TikTok say no. 170 Nguyễn Văn Lộc. Use the Maps link.
- **Hadu Sushi:** the fanpage and website are for the chain and were taken from a listicle, not verified for this branch.
- **Shogun:** the Facebook link (ShogunStreetBBQ) comes from the brand website's footer.
- **Ippudo:** no verified fanpage, so facebook is null.
- **Opening hours:** many venues outside the mall split lunch and dinner. Dinner usually starts at 17:00–17:30 and the kitchen closes at 21:30–22:00. Nama and SAKU close at 21:30. Plan the CGV showtime around this.
- **Price bands** are Google's crowd-sourced "per person" ranges. Buffet prices are before VAT and service charge.
